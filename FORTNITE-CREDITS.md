Credits
This game was made in ~700 hours in the span of 4 months.
It was put together entirely by me, FunnyAnimatorJimTV, 
including all pieces of art and programming, all done in
Scratch. However, I had many sources of inspiration, ideas
and concepts from others, and this game would not be in its
current state without these sources. Here are some of them:
Of course I must credit Fortnite, made by Epic Games, and
also ZombsRoyale.io, which I encountered halfway through
development and gave me many ideas and improvements.
I also must thank my amazing friends, @Light_Stuido and
@SaltyLizard96. I noticed them in late 2019 and their battle
games inspired and made me wonder about possibilities for
refining the intense gameplay. Their encouragement and
support was the main source of fuel for my adventure with
this game - it would have not existed especially without them.
